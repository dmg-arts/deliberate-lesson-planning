    About

    Free, open source, and built to keep working when the network doesn't.

    • Works offline. Install it and the whole app, including PDF export, runs with no connection.
    • No accounts, no tracking, no server. Your lesson plans never leave your device unless you send them.
    • Zero dependencies. The PDF writer is part of this app; nothing is loaded from anyone else.
    • Blank plans print. Export without filling anything in to get a paper worksheet.
